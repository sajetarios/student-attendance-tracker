Data protection scope

Master-data replication does not copy staff or student passwords, login sessions, parent accounts or contact numbers, student QR tokens, learner or teacher photos, device credentials, SMS outbox records, or locally stored SF2 files. Confirmed daily-absence overrides are not included in this snapshot. Newly created cloud staff mirrors are non-login identities; existing cloud account credentials are not overwritten. Attendance events are sent separately through the retry queue.
